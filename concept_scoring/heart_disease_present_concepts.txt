Ischemic Chest Pain – exertional, substernal, relieved by rest.
Exercise-Induced Angina – pain or ECG changes triggered by stress.
ST Segment Depression – measurable drop during exercise.
Abnormal ECG Morphology – flat/downsloping ST, LV hypertrophy, T-wave inversion.
Reduced Exercise Capacity – low METs, early fatigue.
Abnormal Myocardial Perfusion – fixed or reversible thallium defect.
Wall Motion Abnormality – reduced or asynchronous contraction.
Elevated Cardiometabolic Risk – high cholesterol, hypertension, diabetes.
Atherosclerotic Burden – ≥1 major vessel narrowed by >50%.
Demographic Predisposition – older male, smoker, family history.
